# MV Composer -- Remotion + i2v Video Generation (Horizontal & Vertical)

A skill that combines Remotion (React-based video framework) and Kling 3.0 i2v to automatically generate promotional MV videos and viral short videos.

**Primary uses**:
- **Horizontal (16:9)**: Service/course promotional MVs, SNS ad videos, LP embedded videos
- **Vertical (9:16)**: TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels
- **Square (1:1)**: Instagram Feed

For the complete production workflow, viral techniques cheat sheet, quality checklist, video QA pipeline, file structure, and React MG scene templates, refer to the original SKILL.md.
